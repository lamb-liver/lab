## Figure

The parabola $y^2 = 4px$ opens to the right. The focus is $F = (p, 0)$. Rays leave $F$, meet the curve, and continue horizontally to the right, parallel to the axis.

$$
y^2 = 4px
$$

- $p$ is the focal length, the distance from the vertex to the focus $F = (p, 0)$
- At a point $P(x, y)$ on the curve, the reflected ray has direction $(1, 0)$, to the right along the $x$-axis

## Interaction

- **Focal length p**: change how wide the parabola opens; the focus moves with $p$
- **Ray count**: add or remove the rays that leave the focus
- **Scan speed ω**: change how fast the hit points stretch up and down together; the reflected rays stay horizontal

## What to notice

- Rays that leave the focus are parallel to each other after they reflect.
- Changing $p$ moves the opening and the focus. The reflected rays stay parallel; they do not gather to a point.
- During the scan, every reflected ray stays parallel to the axis while the hit heights stretch and shrink together.
