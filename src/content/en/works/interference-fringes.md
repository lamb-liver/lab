## Figure

Two sources stand a distance $d$ apart. The path difference from a point to the two sources decides, in the formula, whether the waves meet in phase or out of phase. The figure draws the in-phase curves, the bright fringes $\Delta r = n\lambda$. For $n \neq 0$ those curves are hyperbolas:

$$
x = a\cosh t = a\frac{e^t + e^{-t}}{2},\quad
y = b\sinh t = b\frac{e^t - e^{-t}}{2}
$$

- **d** is the distance between the sources. **λ** is the wavelength and sets how far apart the orders sit. An order is drawn only when $|n|\lambda < d$.
- **2a = nλ** is the path difference, with $n$ the order of a bright fringe.
- **b = √(c² − a²)** and **c = d/2**. The order $n = 0$ is the perpendicular bisector between the sources.

## Interaction

- **Source distance d**: change the distance between the sources. The hyperbolas open or close, the number of orders changes, and the curves grow out again from the middle.
- **Wavelength λ**: change the spacing of the orders. The curves then grow out again from the middle.
- **Time speed ω**: advance the phase. The drawn curve oscillates about the fixed curve of that order.

## What to notice

- The bright fringes of the formula are the curves $\Delta r = n\lambda$, including the straight order $n = 0$ on the perpendicular bisector.
- Source distance d and wavelength λ together decide which orders fit, since an order is drawn only when $|n|\lambda < d$.
- With the sources symmetric, the pattern is symmetric about the perpendicular bisector.
