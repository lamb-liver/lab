## Figure

The rose curve has the form:

$$
r = a\cos(k\theta)
$$

When k is an integer, an odd k gives k petals and an even k gives 2k petals.

## Interaction

- **Petal count k**: drag the slider to an integer k. An odd k gives k petals and an even k gives 2k petals. Changing k grows the curve out from the center again

## What to notice

- $r=a\cos(k\theta)$ draws symmetric petals in polar coordinates.
- The amplitude $a$ only changes the scale. The number of petals and the symmetry are set by k.
- Where the petals meet, $\cos(k\theta)=0$.
